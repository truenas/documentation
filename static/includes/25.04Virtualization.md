{{< hint type=important icon=gdoc_info_outline title="Virtual Machines and Containers in TrueNAS 25.10" >}}

TrueNAS 25.04 introduced support for [**Containers**]({{< ref "/scaletutorials/containers/" >}}) (named Instances in pre-25.04.2 releases), enabling lightweight isolation similar to jails in FreeBSD-based TrueNAS.

In TrueNAS 25.04.2 and later, virtual machines are created and appear on the [**Virtual Machines**]({{< ref "/scaletutorials/virtualmachines/" >}}) screen.
Legacy VMs created in 25.04.0 or 25.04.1 using the instances feature and some VMs migrated to those versions from TrueNAS 24.10 continue to function and appear on the **Containers** screen.
Legacy VMs on the **Containers** screen do not autostart in 25.10 or later.

Users with existing VMs on the **Containers** screen should consider migrating associated zvols to the **Virtual Machines** screen at this time to ensure compatibility with future TrueNAS releases.
For information on migrating your zvol storage volume to a new VM created in 25.10 or later, see **[Migrating Legacy VMs to Virtual Machines]({{< ref "/SCALETutorials/VirtualMachines/#migrating-legacy-vms-to-virtual-machines" >}})**.

Due to backend technology changes in TrueNAS 27, containers on the **Containers** screen require migration when you upgrade from TrueNAS 25.10.
On TrueNAS Enterprise High Availability (HA) systems, containers do not migrate automatically.
On non-HA TrueNAS Enterprise systems, containers migrate automatically when the Apps license feature is enabled, and do not when it is not enabled.
Contact [TrueNAS Enterprise Support](https://www.truenas.com/support/) if you have containers on a TrueNAS Enterprise system that does not migrate them.

Legacy VMs on the **Containers** screen are not migrated automatically and are removed from the **Containers** screen in TrueNAS 27.
Complete the steps in [Preparing to Upgrade to TrueNAS 27]({{< ref "/SCALETutorials/VirtualMachines/#preparing-to-upgrade-to-truenas-27" >}}) before you upgrade.
